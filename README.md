### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **2nd Year Computer Science & Engineering student** at **Amal Jyothi College of Engineering** (Class of 2029) based in Kerala, India. I engineer software across **edge AI, computer vision, autonomous robotics, and stochastic simulation engines**—building from hardware and physical constraints up.

* 🤖 **Autonomous Robotics & Edge AI:** On-device ThermalNet YOLO human detection (<20ms latency) on Qualcomm RB5, off-grid LoRa peer-to-peer telemetry meshes, and ROS 2 autonomous navigation for campus electric vehicles.
* 🏎️ **Simulation & Systems:** Vectorized 10,000-pass Monte Carlo race strategy engines, non-linear physical degradation modeling, and offline-first mobile architectures in Kotlin.
* 🌐 **Tactile Web & Graphics:** Hardware-accelerated 60 FPS Canvas 2D/WebGL engines, custom physics components, and fluid interactive interfaces.
